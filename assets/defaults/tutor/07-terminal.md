# 7. The terminal

Dust runs nothing from a project until you trust it: `:trust` and
`:untrust`.

    ctrl-/        open or hide the terminal at the bottom

In the terminal everything goes to the shell. `esc esc` switches to
Normal mode to scroll and copy with `v` and `y`; `i` types again.
`ctrl-k` goes to the window above.

`:taskrun`, `:test` and `:tasks` run the project's tasks and tests.
